Research

Repairing compressed representations
Quantisation, pruning and distillation each damage an embedding space in a different way. Distillation mostly rotates it, so a closed-form Procrustes alignment recovers about half of the lost accuracy; quantisation noise does not yield to rotation. I also study why optimal transport fails at the same repair.
Geometry for reliable retrieval
Retrieved passages that mix topics form a graph with low-curvature bridges. Ollivier–Ricci curvature and spectral Ricci flow on that graph cut retrieval topic-mismatch by 54% in a controlled study, without fine-tuning.
Interpretable physics-informed models
In Kolmogorov–Arnold Hamiltonian networks trained from pixels, richer symbolic bases fit the learned field better but do not simulate the dynamics more stably.
